Our Story

Freedom Cleaning was founded by a veteran who believes your solar investment deserves to be treated with the same care and precision drilled into every service member. After seeing too many rushed, low-quality jobs around town, we set out to do it differently.

Today we specialize in solar panel cleaning, bird proofing, and full exterior care for homes and businesses. About a quarter of our work is fixing other companies' mistakes — and we take pride in making bad work right.

We're fully licensed and insured, and every job is done the safe, documented way. No shortcuts, no surprises, just honest work that protects your property and your investment.
